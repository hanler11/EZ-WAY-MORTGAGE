Mortgage Options for New Jersey Borrowers

  • Purchase loans: Conventional, FHA, VA, USDA Rural Housing, and Jumbo programs, subject to program rules.
  • Refinancing: Rate-and-term, cash-out, and FHA or VA streamline options when available.
  • Home equity: HELOC and other home-equity options, subject to lender requirements.
  • Investment-property financing: DSCR and alternative income-documentation options when available and appropriate for the property and borrower.
  • Borrower coaching: Credit and pre-approval coaching, loan-program guidance, and loan-processing support.

Whether you are a first-time buyer, repeat buyer, self-employed borrower, investor, growing family, or homeowner considering a refinance, the right next step depends on your goals and the lender's guidelines.
